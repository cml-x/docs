Browse and manage your project's **configurations** — plain settings as well as secrets — across
every [template](/config-sources/templates) in one place.

## What you can do

- **Full-text search** across configuration names, aliases and templates.
- Browse **per-template**, or across everything with the "all configurations" view.
- **Edit or delete** an existing configuration.
- **Re-distribute** a configuration to additional environments/targets after creation, without
  recreating it.
