title: Templates
sidebar_position: 4
---

A **Config Template** defines what a configuration of a given kind looks like: its schema (fields,
types, validation, enum choices, defaults), and whether it's inherently sensitive.

Templates are what drive the dynamic form you fill in under
[Getting Started](/config-sources/getting-start) — pick the template that matches what you're
configuring (a database credential, an API key, an app settings file…) and the right fields appear
automatically.
