Organization-wide security policies, grouped into three areas.

## Login Policy

Password login, external IdP login, self-registration, passwordless login, MFA enforcement,
password-reset link visibility, and unknown-user handling for email login.

## Password Policy

Complexity rules new passwords must satisfy.

## Lockout Policy

Failed sign-in attempt thresholds before an account is temporarily locked.

See also [MFA Policy](/access/mfa-policy) for second-factor-specific rules, kept separate from
these general settings.
