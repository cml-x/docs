---
title: Identity Providers
sidebar_position: 4
---

Federate an external identity provider so users can sign in with an account they already have,
instead of a platform-specific password.

## Supported providers

Google, GitHub, GitHub Enterprise, Microsoft (Azure AD), GitLab (cloud or self-hosted), Apple,
generic **OIDC**, or **SAML 2.0**.

## Managing a provider

Once configured, a provider can be **activated**, **deactivated**, or **deleted** independently of
the others — turning one off doesn't affect users who sign in through a different provider or a
platform password.
