**Authorizations** grant a [user](/access/users) access to a specific project.

## Granting access

Pick a user, a project, and one or more of that project's defined roles. A grant can be
**activated**, **deactivated**, or **deleted** at any time — access is revoked immediately when a
grant is turned off, without needing to remove the user's account.

This is separate from the organization-level admin roles assigned under [Users](/access/users):
authorizations are strictly project-scoped.
