title: Manage the Versions of an Asset
sidebar_position: 3
---

See the versions of an asset, what changed in each, and choose the one to use.

## Before you begin

- An asset with at least one version.

## Procedure

{/* screenshot: library-tasks-manage-versions */}

1. Open the asset in **Assets Explorer**.
2. Open its **Version Manager**.
3. Read what each version changed, then choose the one to use.

## Good to know

- Use a fixed version in test and production, so that what you tested is what runs.
- A major version announces a breaking change: read its notes before moving to it.
